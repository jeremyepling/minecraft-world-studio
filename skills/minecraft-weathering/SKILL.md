---
name: minecraft-weathering
description: Apply architectural weathering to registered masonry when a builder asks for age, rain streaks, damp foundations or window drips.
---

Trace moisture from a feature that collects or sheds water. Below an opening, place the darkest stone against the sill, then soften it down the face. Scale the streak to the height of the building. Keep nearby wall areas quiet so the opening reads from a distance.

In Studio, use drips=true for window streaks or a general weathering request on a windowed tower. Use drips=false to remove streaks. Keep drips=null for unrelated edits. A river bridge has a separate waterline palette. Preserve its strength unless the request changes it. An observed opening needs exposed masonry below it for a visible streak. Review from the same camera and protect manual material changes.
