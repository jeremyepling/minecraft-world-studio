# Color and materials

Squint at the facade, or compare a grayscale view. A roof that merges with the hillside may need a different value mass before it needs another hue. Place the widest value interval at the focal point and reserve a narrower interval for supporting masonry.

For an aged warm wall, try an earthy shadow, sand midtone and pale warm accent. A cool stone wall might instead move from muted blue-gray shelter to a neutral light plane. A copper roof can act as the single cooler accent in a predominantly warm composition. Check compatibility on side and top faces under the active pack. Large brick joints or a striped end grain can defeat an otherwise close color match. Falling sand, ticking copper, luminous blocks and block entities have functional consequences.

Work at the scale of the surface. Sketch connected patches across a bay or buttress, then vary their edges by a few blocks. Lower local contrast and medium-scale variation first when a player calls a surface noisy. Keep the broad value design. Independent sampling tends to give every stone equal visual weight. Check a small swatch beside the adjacent roof and timber before adopting a new palette.

The three shipped masonry materials are curated authored ramps. Pack-specific color measurements, alternative block shapes and automatically constructed hue ramps are later capabilities.
