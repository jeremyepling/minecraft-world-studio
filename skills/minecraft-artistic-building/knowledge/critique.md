# Critique and continuity

Start at the player's primary view. At long distance evaluate skyline and separation from the background. At medium distance compare the main opening, the roof and the supporting wall for attention. Up close inspect seams, isolated high-contrast blocks, directional states and whether drip origins make sense. For repeat comparisons keep camera, seed, resource packs, FOV, weather and time fixed. A second angle tests whether the improvement survives walking around it.

Give a concrete observation, its effect on the eye and one change a builder could make. For example, a bright strip around the full cap can draw attention away from a doorway. Narrow the contrast on the supporting wall and compare from the road again. A crop or single frame gives evidence for only the surfaces in view. With no image, give a recipe or spatial assessment and identify what remains to be seen.

Scope a refinement to the chosen material, region or geometry parameter. A darker roof preserves the shaft finish. A taller tower keeps the same material ramp, texture strength and focal intent unless the player changes them. An explicit style match creates an independent local copy. Ask whether a preference is project-wide only when the difference matters. A one-off trial belongs in a preview or undoable local revision.
