# Known limitations

* **Conversation and providers.** The listener launches a fresh Codex CLI process for design interpretation. Short local context and saved edit history cover some follow-ups. Persistent provider sessions, configurable alternative runtimes, voice input/output and general redirection are Milestone 1 work. [Listener](../scripts/chat-loop.mjs) · [Milestone](current-milestone.md)
* **Spatial selection.** Recipe-based revisions know the generated tower geometry. Independent live selection currently recognizes an isolated overhanging roof that narrows above a shaft, subject to material and scan limits. A connected wing, an ambiguous eave or a stateful block may cause it to stop. There is no general architectural segmentation or world-scale planning model. [Survey](../scripts/survey-roof.mjs)
* **Creative control.** A particle outline shows massing. Checked writes and undo protect divergent states. Comparing block states cannot identify who placed a matching block. Interrupted multi-column work can be partial. Transactions, redo and ghost-block alternatives are future work. A surveyed recolor has its own undo receipt and does not update the recipe. [Recovery](in-game-building.md)
* **Visual and manual evidence.** Current views come from the Minecraft framebuffer. Opposite-side survey photos used the player camera and restored the original pose. Independent rendering and a human Axiom collaboration trial remain open. [Survey photo](../screenshots/survey-roof-southeast-tower.png) · [Manual test coverage](playtest.md)
* **World and performance.** The supported workspace is one singleplayer Creative Overworld. Save names can collide across copies, and column-by-column guarded writes cost more round trips than a bulk fill. Use the private instance and its matching state for recovery.
