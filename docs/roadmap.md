# Product roadmap

The sequence follows the product vision. The success checks describe experiences to demonstrate, without assigning release dates.

| Milestone | Capability | Success check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · In-game collaboration** | Persistent conversation, capable agent and speech providers, push-to-talk, optional speech, spatial references, markers, activity reporting, interruption and reliable testing. | Spend an hour comfortably building with the AI inside Minecraft. [Milestone 1](milestone-1.md) |
| **2 · Building fluency** | A composable geometry SDK, parametric components, curves, extrusions, lofts, sweeps, booleans, schematic execution and offline geometry tests. | Completed November 25, 2026. Towers and curved bridges passed in-game revision, preservation and undo checks. [Acceptance results](milestone-2.md) |
| **3 · Artistic intelligence** | Artistic skills, texture and color analysis, palette references, weathering, terrain blending, critique and learned preferences. | In progress. Stored art direction, painterly masonry, weathering and visual critique now work on the initial building workflow. Next is a bounded editable facade with a correctable surface mask. [Phased plan](painterly-building.md) |
| **4 · Creative control** | Design alternatives, ghost previews, persistent object identities, revisions, conflict detection, rollback and independent inspection. | Refine a complex structure over many iterations while preserving manual work. |
| **5 · World-scale creative studio** | Regional surveys and maps, persistent district plans, infrastructure, architectural consistency and planning overlays. | Develop a coherent world together across weeks or months. |

Future possibilities include multiplayer, multiple agents, full-duplex voice, survival assistance, additional loaders, Litematica blueprints, public distribution and shared libraries or style packs. A concrete need and an acceptance check should precede implementation. [Full roadmap and product principles](product-vision.md)
